## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored copy poured into an unmodified generic-DTC template. The words know Jaipur; the visual system does not. Outfit, one desaturated rose on off-white, lettermark "M", every section is the canonical template block in canonical order. No kesar/pista/vark palette, no mithai-only interaction (no weight selector, no box builder despite the hero CTA).

Deterministic scan: CLI detector DEGRADED (parser modules missing, npm unreachable), regex fallback 0 findings (undercount). In-page overlay: 9 low-contrast 1.0:1 hits on gift-box tile text, all false positives (photo + gradient scrim behind text ignored by the tool).

Objective measurements: 0 unlabelled interactive elements; 0 images without alt; 2 texts under 12px (count badges, 11px); universal :focus-visible verified; 1 text typeface; radii 999/12/8; 6 text colours; no horizontal overflow at 375px; 58 of 71 visible tap targets under 44x44 at mobile (chips 38px, Add 38px, hearts 36px, footer links 24px tall, qty 32px, Remove 20px tall).

## Priority Issues

[P0] Hero photo is not the product (chakli), caption says "Jalebi", alt says "Assorted Indian sweets on a brass tray". Fix: real jalebi/Kaju Katli hero; caption and alt match; concrete time caption. Command: /impeccable delight.

[P1] Payment radios are decorative (no handler); checkout has zero reassurance (no PIN serviceability, no cold-chain note, no contact/damage policy). Fix: CTA restates consequence per method; UPI ID field or redirect note; fixed reassurance row above CTA; demo PIN check. Command: /impeccable harden.

[P1] "-" at qty 1 deletes the line with no confirmation or undo; "-" and "Remove" are 8px apart. Fix: disable "-" at 1; toast with 5s Undo on removal. Command: /impeccable harden.

[P2] Mobile: shop header eats first viewport (~200px of controls); checkout dialog hides Total and CTA ~290px below the fold with no cue; 58 tap targets under 44px. Fix: single-row scrolling chips, sort as icon button; sticky summary+CTA in dialog or bottom sheet; lift every target to 44px. Command: /impeccable adapt.

[P2] Business rules printed but not enforced: min 50 sells at 1; ETA copy inconsistent; "handwritten card" vs "Gift message"; no qty ceiling; "Build a gift box" CTA leads to fixed SKUs. Fix: min/step 50; city-derived ETA; rename + echo message on success; cap qty; builder or rename CTA. Command: /impeccable harden, /impeccable clarify.
